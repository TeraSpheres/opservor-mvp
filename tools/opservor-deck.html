The gap

A question your systems cannot answer

“Which of my depots is going to struggle next Thursday, and why?”

Your warehouse system knows the docks are filling up. Your fleet system knows two vehicles are booked in for service. Your stock system knows an item is running down.

All three are correct. None of them talk to each other — so the answer only arrives on Thursday, when somebody spends the afternoon firefighting and calls it bad luck.

28 is above 26.
So it says you are fine.

That is the whole of the logic — one number compared with another. It does not know how fast the item is leaving. It does not know how long your supplier takes. It has never once divided one by the other.

It is not fine. Seven units a day are leaving. 28 ÷ 7 = four days of stock. Your supplier takes ten. Ordering this morning still leaves six days with an empty shelf — and the screen above will show green on every one of them.

Trust

Every finding shows its working

An operations manager will not act on “trust me, Thursday will fall over” — and should not. So the arithmetic is on the page, not behind a support ticket.

  1. Count what actually left758 units shipped over the last 90 days. Not a forecast — that happened.
  2. Turn it into a rate758 ÷ 90 = 7 units a day.
  3. Work out what is really yours46 on hand, 18 already promised to orders, so 28 can actually be sold.
  4. Divide28 ÷ 7 = 4 days of cover.
  5. Compare against the supplierFour is less than ten. You are not approaching a problem — you are six days late.

What it will not do

Most software in this market is sold on what it might eventually do. This is what Opservor does not do today, stated before you ask.

  • It does not predict the future.It divides what has happened by how fast it is happening. That is arithmetic, not prophecy.
  • It does not know your supplier lead times.Nothing records them yet, so it assumes ten days — and says so on every finding that relies on it.
  • It does not replace your warehouse or fleet system.It reads them. Keep what you have; this joins it up.
  • It has no customers yet.You would be the first. That is a real risk and it is priced into the conversation.

A supplier who tells you the limits before you sign is a supplier who will tell you when something breaks.
